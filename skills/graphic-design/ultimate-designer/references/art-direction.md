# Art direction and brand system

Use the supplied company guide as the authority, not a generated mood board. If no guide exists yet, propose options explicitly as provisional and request approval before presenting them as the brand.

## Direction sheet

For each proposed direction record: concept in one sentence, audience and message, typographic hierarchy, palette roles (background, text, accent), grid and margins, image/3D/vector treatment, distinctive motif, and why it fits the content. Avoid treating "sleek" as a complete brief; explain the actual visual decisions. Explore multiple compositions rather than simply changing colors on the same template.

Use design tokens for repeated decisions:

- **Primitive:** approved color values, font families and weights, spacing and type scales.
- **Semantic:** background, text, accent, divider, highlight, warning, photo treatment.
- **Component:** product card, spec table, sticker label, callout, price, page furniture.

Permit intentional per-format overrides, but keep the brand's master decisions identifiable. A poster may have a different scale from a catalog without becoming a different brand.

## Format-specific checks

- **Catalog:** vary editorial pacing (hero, grid, detail, comparison) while keeping a predictable product-data hierarchy. Dense does not mean tiny type.
- **Flyer/poster:** choose a focal message and an appropriate viewing distance; preserve required details and legible contrast.
- **Sticker:** prefer simple silhouettes and legible shapes at physical size; keep artwork, white border, and vendor-specific cut path separate.
- **Individual visual component:** define its dimensions, intended neighbors, reusable variants, editable elements, and export format.

Review against the approved guide: correct logo variant and clear space, approved colors and fonts, consistent imagery and tone, readable contrast, and accurate copy. Keep typography and logos out of generated imagery when they need to be editable or exact. Check commercial rights for fonts, reference images, and generated assets before release.
